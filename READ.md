# README --- NovaLend Product Owner Decision Kit

**Author:** Abbas Abdulsamad\
**Year:** 2026\
**Project:** NovaLend --- FirstBank Digital Factory

## Overview

The NovaLend Product Owner Decision Kit presents a data-driven product
strategy for improving the NovaLend lending beta, reducing credit risk,
and preparing for the six-week CBN reporting deadline.

## Key Recommendation

Proceed with a **restricted launch** rather than a full rollout or a
complete pause. The restricted launch is intended to reduce default risk
while allowing the product to continue progressing toward regulatory and
business objectives.

## Key Measures

-   Reduce the maximum initial loan amount from ₦50,000 to ₦15,000.
-   Strengthen underwriting and block high-risk Tier 4 applicants.
-   Require automated repayment setup, including NIBSS Direct Debit
    where applicable.
-   Introduce dynamic loan amounts and flexible repayment tenures.

## Success Metrics

-   Reduce the 30-day default rate from 14% to **5.8% or lower**.
-   Increase offer acceptance among eligible Tier 1 and Tier 2
    borrowers.
-   Ensure mandatory auto-debit enrollment for accepted loans.

## Risk Controls

At Week 4 after restrictions are introduced, freeze new loan
disbursements if the 14-day rolling PAR-14 delinquency rate exceeds
7.5%, or if the CBN rejects the revised risk policy during pre-filing
review. The proposed fallback is collection-only mode while seeking a
regulatory extension or temporary freeze.

## Expected Outcome

A controlled, measurable launch that prioritizes portfolio quality,
regulatory readiness, repayment reliability, and sustainable growth.
